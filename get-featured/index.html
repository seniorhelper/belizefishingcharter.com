What “Featured” means here

  • Top placement: your fishing charter is shown near the top of the flats, reef and deep-sea charter sections on Belize Fishing Charter.
  • A clear badge: every paid spot is labeled Featured, so readers know it’s a placement.
  • A photo: one image of your boat, crew, site or guests in action.
  • Your booking link: visitors go straight to your site or booking page. Paid links carry rel="sponsored", as search engines require.

Questions owners ask

Is there a free option?

Yes. A basic listing on Belize Fishing Charter is free for fishing guides and charters serving San Pedro and Ambergris Caye. We verify every operator first. Featured is an optional upgrade at $29 a month.

What exactly do I get with Featured?

A top spot in the flats, reef and deep-sea charter sections on Belize Fishing Charter, a Featured badge, one photo, a short pitch in your words and a direct link to your booking page. You can swap the photo or pitch whenever your season or offer changes.

Will Featured guarantee bookings?

No. We promise the placement and the work behind it, not a number of bookings. Visitors still decide based on your reviews, price, safety record and how easy you are to book.

How is a paid spot labeled?

Every paid placement shows a visible Featured badge, and the link to your site is marked rel="sponsored". That keeps the guide honest for readers and within search engine rules, and it protects your own site too.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime online. Your spot stays live through the month you’ve paid for and then returns to a basic listing.
